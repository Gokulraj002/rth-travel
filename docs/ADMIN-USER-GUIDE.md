# Admin panel: guide for the team

Sign in at **https://admin.yourdomain.com**. The menu on the left only shows what your role allows. Items with a small
**Pro** or **Premium** lock belong to a higher plan. Click one to see what it does and how to upgrade.

---

## Everyday flow: from enquiry to happy traveller

**1. An enquiry arrives.** Website enquiries appear in **Enquiries** automatically. Add phone or walk-in leads with **New enquiry**.
With Pro, the person is also saved under **Customers** (matched by phone number, so there are no duplicates).

**2. Work the lead** *(Pro)*. **Lead pipeline** shows every lead as a card in a column:
*New → Contacted → Quote sent → Negotiation → Won / Lost*. Drag a card to move it, or use the small menu on the card (phone).
Open a lead to log a call or WhatsApp chat, assign it to a team member, and set a **follow-up** date.
**Follow-ups** lists everything due today and anything overdue. The dashboard reminds you too.

**3. Send a quotation** *(Pro)*. From the lead, click **Create quotation**, or go to **Quotations → New**.
- Pick the customer, or add a new one with just a name and phone number.
- Choose a package and click **Load itinerary**: the days, inclusions and exclusions fill in by themselves. Edit freely.
- Add price lines (hotel, cab, activities…), a discount and GST. Totals calculate automatically.
- **Send on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with a ready message and a link to a beautiful online quotation the customer can open on their phone.
  **Print / Save PDF** gives the same document as a PDF.

**4. Customer says yes: confirm the booking** *(Pro)*. On the quotation, click **Convert to booking**. Everything is copied over.
Add the travellers' names, the pickup point and any special requests. **Send confirmation on WhatsApp** sends the customer a confirmation message.

**5. Record payments** *(Pro)*. On the booking, click **Record payment** (UPI, cash, bank…). The paid amount and balance
update instantly. Print a **receipt** or send a "payment received" WhatsApp message.

**6. Operations** *(Premium)*. Add the hotels, houseboats and cabs used in the booking under **Costs & profit**. You see
the real **profit** on every trip and how much you owe each vendor. **Vehicles & drivers** warns you before insurance or permits expire.

**7. GST invoice** *(Premium)*. On the booking, click **Create GST invoice**. CGST and SGST (same state) or IGST (other state) are calculated
for you. Print it or save it as a PDF.

**8. Before the trip.** **Trip calendar** shows all departures for the month. Send a **trip reminder** from the booking.

## Other sections
| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Today's numbers: new enquiries, bookings, money collected, balance due, follow-ups and alerts |
| **Customers** *(Pro)* | Everyone you've dealt with: trips, total spent, birthdays, notes |
| **Payments** *(Pro)* | All money received, by date and method, with totals |
| **Reports** *(Pro)* | Sales, collections, outstanding balances, lead sources, top packages, plus profit and GST summary (Premium) |
| **Packages, Destinations, Reviews, FAQs** | What the public website shows. Saving updates the website within seconds |
| **Website settings** | Phone numbers, WhatsApp, emails, addresses and social links shown on the website |
| **Business details** | Legal name, GSTIN, bank and UPI details, document prefixes and terms, used on quotations and invoices |
| **Message templates** *(Premium)* | The ready-made WhatsApp and email texts. `{{customer_name}}`-style words are filled in automatically |
| **Team & roles** | Add staff. *Owner* and *Editor* on every plan; *Manager*, *Sales* and *Accounts* with Premium |
| **Activity log** *(Premium)* | Who changed what, and when |
| **Backup & export** *(Premium)* | Download a full backup, or Excel-ready lists of customers, bookings, payments… |
| **Plan & upgrade** *(owner)* | Your current plan, a plan comparison and the box for your license key |

## Staff roles *(Premium)*
- **Owner**: everything, including the team, plan and backups.
- **Manager**: everything except the team, license and backups.
- **Sales**: enquiries, lead pipeline, customers, quotations and bookings.
- **Accounts**: payments, invoices, vendors, profit and reports.
- **Editor**: website content, settings and enquiries.

## Good habits
- Fill in **Business details** first (GSTIN, bank, UPI), so every quotation and invoice is correct.
- Download a **backup** every week (Backup & export, or Hostinger backups).
- Give each staff member their own login. Never share the owner password.
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes. That is a safety feature, not a fault.
